Scholarships
RMIT University Vietnam provides a range of scholarships to promising students every year.
So far, we have awarded more than 613 billion VND worth of scholarships to more than 1,900 young people from all over Vietnam and around the world, who exemplify the kinds of characteristics that RMIT prides itself on.
Explore your options and apply today, you’ve got nothing to lose and everything to gain.

How to apply for a scholarship
Before applying, we recommend reading the
scholarship terms and conditions
.
1. Find your program
2. Check your eligibility
3. Prepare your documents
4. Submit your application
Each scholarship has unique requirements, find the full details by researching each scholarship.
RMIT Vietnam reserves the right to adjust the values and allocation of the scholarships at its discretion.
The scholarship application process is highly competitive with a limited number of scholarships available. Unfortunately, not all eligible applicants will receive one.
