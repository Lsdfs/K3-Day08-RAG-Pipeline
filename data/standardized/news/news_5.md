# RMIT University News Article 5

> Source: https://www.rmit.edu.vn/news | Date: 2026-08-05 | Category: Student Services

## Campus Updates and Student Services

RMIT Vietnam is pleased to announce several updates to student services for the current academic year. The university continues to enhance its support infrastructure across all campuses.

## Library Services Expansion

The university library has expanded its digital collection significantly. Students now have access to over 500,000 e-books, academic journals, and research databases through the online portal. Physical study spaces have been upgraded with additional power outlets and high-speed Wi-Fi connectivity. The library now offers extended hours during examination periods, remaining open until midnight.

## Financial Aid and Scholarships

The financial aid office has announced new scholarship opportunities for both domestic and international students. Merit-based scholarships covering up to 50 percent of tuition fees are available for high-achieving students. Need-based financial aid applications are processed on a rolling basis throughout the academic year.

## Student Life and Campus Events

Upcoming campus events include career fairs, guest lectures from industry professionals, and cultural festivals celebrating diversity at RMIT. Students are encouraged to participate in extracurricular activities and student clubs to enhance their university experience. The student services office provides information and registration for all events through the online portal.
