# University Policy Document

> Source: RMIT Vietnam | Retrieved: 2026-08-04

## 1. Student Services Overview
RMIT Vietnam provides comprehensive student services including academic advising, career counseling, and mental health support. All services are available to enrolled students free of charge.

## 2. Library Resources and Access
The RMIT library system offers access to over 500,000 digital resources including academic journals, e-books, and research databases. Students can access these resources 24/7 through the online portal. Study spaces are available at both Saigon South and Hanoi campuses.

## 3. Course Registration and Academic Calendar
Students register for courses through the online student portal. Registration periods are announced at least two weeks before opening. Students must meet prerequisite requirements and have no outstanding fees to register.

## 4. Fees and Financial Information
Tuition fees are due before the start of each semester. Payment plans are available for eligible students. Late payment penalties apply after the due date. Financial aid and scholarship applications are processed through the financial services office.
