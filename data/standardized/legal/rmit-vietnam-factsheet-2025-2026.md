Academic information
The academic calendar is available here.
A list of course offerings for each semester can be found in the Course Search Tool.
All courses at RMIT Vietnam are offered in English.
Each course at RMIT is typically worth 12 credits. A full-time study load consists of 3-4 courses (36-
48 credits).
Upon completing the study, students will be able to access the official and verified digital transcript
via the MyeQuals platform and share it with their home institutions.
Visa information
Visa type: DH - student visa is for study purposes only. International students holding a student visa
are not allowed to engage in work or internship activities without the institution’s permission.
Process: Students can expect to receive a Visa Invitation Letter and detailed instructions on how to
apply for a student visa approximately two months before the semester starts.
More information about visa can be found here.

Cost and fees to come on exchange
Tuition fees: Students enrolled in the exchange program do not pay tuition fees to RMIT University but
continue to pay fees to their home institutions.
Health insurance is mandatory for all international students while studying at RMIT Vietnam.
Cost of living
The estimated monthly expense ranges from $600 - $1,000, including bills:
Rent: $300 - $600
Food: $200 - $300
Utilities: $50 - $70
Transportation: $50 - $70
